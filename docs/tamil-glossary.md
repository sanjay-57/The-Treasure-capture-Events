# Tamil glossary (roadmap 7.1)

One word per concept, so every page, the voice guide and the invites say the same thing. The target is the Tamil a Madurai photographer would use with a family on the phone: written, but close to speech. Use simple verbs and everyday nouns, and address people as நீங்கள் / உங்கள். No literary or Sanskrit-heavy words, and no slang. The full T&C stays formal; only its summaries follow this.

Draft, 21 Oct 2026. **Needs a native-speaker review (ideally the client) before the Phase 7 copy pass.** The counts show how often each word appears in `src/` today, which gives the size of the pass.

## Style rules

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Verbs in the polite imperative: சேருங்கள், தொடுங்கள், சொல்லுங்கள். Never the bare command (சேர், தொடு) in on-screen text; the voice assistant may accept those as input.
- Numbers as digits in text (₹19,999, 4 மணி நேரம்). The voice clips spell them out.
- English loan words that families actually say stay in Tamil script: ஆல்பம், கேண்டிட், ட்ரோன், பிரேம், WhatsApp.
- Brand names stay in Latin script: The Treasure Capture Events, Cradle Captures, Bumpy Tales.
